# Templates

Starting points you fill in: policies, scaffolds, and document shapes that are
worth writing once and adapting.

| Resource | Risk | Use it when |
|---|---|---|
| [`autonomy-policy`](autonomy-policy/) | read-only | Deciding what an agent may do on its own, or writing the `AGENTS.md` section that records it |

## What belongs here

A template ships the *shape* plus the judgement about how to fill it in. A blank
form is not a template; the value is in knowing which blanks matter and what a
bad answer looks like.

## What does not

**Bruin data-source templates belong upstream** in
[bruin-data/bruin](https://github.com/bruin-data/bruin), where they are versioned
with the CLI and scaffolded by `bruin init`. A copy here would be a second thing
to keep correct. Run `bruin init --help` for the live list.
